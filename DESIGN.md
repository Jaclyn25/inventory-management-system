# Stockroom visual system

## Scene and direction
An inventory coordinator works in a bright daytime office and checks shelves on a phone. Light warm surfaces reduce glare contrast between paper labels and the screen. Product register, restrained color strategy.

## Color
Warm stone surfaces in OKLCH, dark brown-gray ink and muted rust for primary actions and selected navigation. Sage indicates available stock, amber indicates low stock, terracotta indicates zero stock. Status labels always carry text.

## Typography and spacing
Native system sans; 30px desktop page title, 17px section title, 14px base, 12px operational tables. Monospace SKU and operation references. Tabular numerals align quantities. A 238px sidebar and 72px header anchor the workspace; content spacing varies from 10 to 36px.

## Components and behavior
Semantic tables with horizontal scrolling inside their region, native modal dialogs, labeled inputs, visible keyboard focus, persistent success messages, inline errors, loading skeletons and instructional empty states. Mobile navigation collapses below 850px. Forms become one column below 550px. Reduced motion is respected.
