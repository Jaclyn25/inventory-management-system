# Stockroom

## Register

product

## Users
Stock coordinators working at desks in brightly lit offices or checking shelves on phones. They need accurate counts and a clear record of every change.

## Product Purpose
Maintain a trustworthy catalog, receive and issue stock, investigate discrepancies and prioritize replenishment. Staff must be able to explain quantities from movement history.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, practical. Direct language and visible outcomes.

## Anti-references
Unfinished CRUD demos, decorative marketing dashboards and unreadable status colors.

## Design Principles
- Show stock health where decisions happen.
- Separate quantity changes from catalog edits.
- Record adjustments to recover from mistakes.
- Put operational detail ahead of decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast target, keyboard access, visible focus, field labels, semantic tables and text alongside status colors.
